# 1.0.0+korboy15

- `screen swipe --from-edge` reports the touch as landing on the nearest
  physical screen edge, the way the touch panel reports a finger that lands
  there. System edge gestures need it to recognize in landscape: without it, a
  swipe up from the bottom edge of a landscape app never started the Home
  gesture. Not available with `--no-momentum` or `--parallel`.

Tested on an iPhone 15 Pro Max with iOS 17.1.1 (rootless), Notes in front:
swipes up from the bottom centre with `--from-edge` at the default speed went
Home in portrait and in both landscape orientations (8 of 8 in landscape, set
with `device rotation set`). Without the flag the same landscape swipes, and
swipes from every other edge, stayed in Notes.
