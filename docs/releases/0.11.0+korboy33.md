- `icli cc list | get | set | toggle` reads and sets Control Center toggles: `airplane`, `wifi`, `bluetooth`, `cellular`, `hotspot`, `airdrop` (`off`, `contacts`, `everyone`; Everyone turns itself off after 10 minutes), `focus` (`on` for Do Not Disturb, `off`, or a Focus name such as `sleep` or `work`), `flashlight` (`on`, `off` or a level from 0 to 1), `rotation-lock`, `low-power` and `appearance` (`light`, `dark`). `mirroring` is read-only: it reports whether the screen is mirrored and to which device. Every command answers with the state read back after the change, and `changed` says whether it moved. A change shows in Control Center and Settings and stays after icli exits; a Focus turned on by icli stays on as one turned on in Control Center does.
- The CLI signing profile adds the entitlements `icli cc` needs: `com.apple.SystemConfiguration.SCPreferences-write-access` (`com.apple.radios.plist`), `com.apple.wifi.manager-access`, `com.apple.bluetooth.system`, `com.apple.private.airdrop.settings`, `com.apple.avfoundation.allow-system-wide-context`, and four Do Not Disturb client-identifier keys. Each is described in docs/entitlements.md.
- While an icli lease is held, the phone shows `icli: <owner>` just above the home indicator, so whoever holds the phone can see which runner is driving it. It is a SpringBoard tweak in the iOS packages, active after a respring. It lets touches through to the app underneath, and icli's own screenshots, OCR and `ui tree` do not see it; a wired display mirror does. It disappears on `lease release` or when the lease lapses. `icli lease indicator off` hides it, `on` brings it back.
- Fork versions are now upstream's version plus `+korboyN`, so they never take a number upstream releases. The previous fork release, first published as 0.12.0, is 0.11.0+korboy30.
- The acceptance suite installs the test apps it needs under its own lease and removes them afterwards, and a stopped run releases its lease.
- `screen scroll-by` and `screen swipe --no-momentum` land where they should over many strokes: a stroke now passes the scroll view's slop a pixel at a time instead of half a point at a time, so the pan starts at the same place every stroke. A 1000-point sideways scroll of six strokes used to land up to 3 points short in about a third of runs; it is now within a point. The stroke takes the same time as before.
- The tvOS build's borrowed IOKit stub gets both tvOS slices whichever iOS slices the iPhoneOS SDK lists, so it builds with Xcode 26.2 and with the release runner's Xcode 26.6, whose stub lists no arm64-ios.
- Acceptance goes to the Home Screen before removing the test app in front, which used to leave the screen black.
- Checked on an iPhone 15 Pro Max running iOS 17.1.1 with Dopamine (rootless): the full acceptance suite without the reboot group passed 103 of 105 cases before the scroll fix (`account_password` needs the mobile user's password and was not run; `touch_scroll_exact` is the scroll fix above). After it, `touch_scroll_exact` passed 10 of 10 runs and the gestures (10 cases) and accessibility (18 cases) groups passed. `make tvos` was built on Xcode 26.2; the release runner builds it on 26.6.
